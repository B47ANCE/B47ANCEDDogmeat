# Native reconstruction provenance

Project Version: 0.2.0-dev.8

Classification is for **complete physical identity continuity through a transition**, not merely one intercepted UI action. SUPPORTED requires demonstrated trusted continuity; RESTRICTABLE requires safe managed-only prevention before execution; UNSUPPORTED means the inspected current boundary cannot authenticate/intercept that path; UNKNOWN means evidence is insufficient. No listed physical transition earns SUPPORTED. Source A anchors are in [the evidence ledger](native-save-load-evidence.json); C anchors retain the [earlier native coverage](NATIVE_ADMISSION_COVERAGE.md).

These are source/offline classifications. Selected action refusals are not actual dedicated-runtime compatibility evidence; RESTRICTABLE for one completion does not certify the whole transport lifecycle or justify CONDITIONAL GO.

The adult/world-only proposal prohibits inventory, transport, hutch, growth and recovery transitions, retains records on absence and refuses ownership. It still requires normal save/load and streaming, which remain unsupported for trusted rebind. Therefore it is **not a technically proven restricted lifecycle**, not CONDITIONAL GO, and not approved as the final gameplay design.

No native replacement, doghouse, equipment or death-recovery gameplay is added. The original future puppy/doghouse design remains in [GROWTH_IDENTITY_FEASIBILITY.md](GROWTH_IDENTITY_FEASIBILITY.md); current limitations do not remove roadmap scope.
