Project Version: 0.2.0-dev.7

M0R assessment, 2026-10-12. Canonical current design rows: [feature_traceability.json](contracts/feature_traceability.json). All historical F01–F101 IDs and requirement meanings are retained, then F102–F132 cover original native foundation and new core sanitation/door/security requirements. Historical source classifications do not transfer to standalone implementation. The older [feature feasibility matrix](FEATURE_FEASIBILITY_MATRIX.md) remains immutable add-on research.

VERIFIED means the narrowly named source/documentation primitive is established; FEASIBLE means an independent design direction; EXPERIMENTAL means native/prototype investigation remains; BLOCKED means no current safe Lua-only path has been established. None means runtime PASS. B-* definitions and stable T-* scenarios are in [the MP matrix](MULTIPLAYER_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md); E-* evidence is in [the source index](M0R_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md). Every row carries authority, status and native evidence metadata in JSON. Dependencies are blockers requiring proof, not third-party mod requirements. F02/F101 retain audit/provenance meaning while their acceptance reflects current standalone scope. Existing row labels may mention the historical original scope; their current evidence/dependencies below are independent.
